# South Brunswick Computer Science Club

**Student-led workshops, projects, and hackathons at South Brunswick High School.**

[Website](https://sbcsclub.vercel.app/) · [Explore Repositories](https://github.com/orgs/sbcsclub/repositories) · [Contact](mailto:csclub@sbschools.org)

---

We're a community of SBHS students learning to code, building projects, and exploring computer science together. Whether you're writing your first program or developing a project of your own, you're welcome here.

## What We Do

- **Workshops:** Learn programming concepts and tools through practical examples, from software development and cloud services to control systems.
- **Projects:** Turn ideas into working software, practice collaborating, and learn from other students.
- **Hackathons:** Work in teams, solve problems, and share what you've built.

## Get Involved

**New to coding?** No prior programming experience is required to join the club. Explore a workshop, ask questions, and build from there.

**Have something to share?** Bring a project idea, help a fellow member, or get in touch about leading a workshop.

Visit our [website](https://sbcsclub.vercel.app/) or email [csclub@sbschools.org](mailto:csclub@sbschools.org) to learn more.
